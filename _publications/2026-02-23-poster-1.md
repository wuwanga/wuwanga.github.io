This work investigates model drift in Tor website fingerprinting by collecting a large-scale, three-month dataset with cache-enabled browsing and exploring Time-series Language Models (TSLMs) for robust traffic classification. It proposes a CNN-based traffic representation that converts Tor traces into feature tokens for TSLM-based analysis.
