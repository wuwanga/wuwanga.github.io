This work investigates privacy leakage from Wi-Fi metadata and presents a lightweight framework for device localization and tracking over existing public Wi-Fi infrastructure. Evaluations across six real-world environments show that the framework achieves sub-meter tracking accuracy in most cases, highlighting significant privacy risks in public Wi-Fi deployments.
