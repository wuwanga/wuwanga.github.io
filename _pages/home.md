I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the [Bellini College of Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, and Computing (CAICC)](https://www.usf.edu/ai-cybersecurity-computing/index.aspx), [University of South Florida (USF)](https://www.usf.edu/). My research focuses on systems security, network security, and AI security, with a particular interest in privacy leakage and side-channel attacks in AI agents, Wi-Fi services, and cloud computing platforms.


Research Interests
======
- System Security
- Network Security
- AI security
